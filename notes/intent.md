# Intent: what this site is for

## Who it's for
- Hiring managers and founders at startups and agencies, plus big-tech teams hiring for platform PM or frontend roles.
- People who skim first: the pages should tell the story without anyone typing into the chat.

## What it should say
- Thesis: "I kept zooming out." Automation → 0→1 → interface → system → platform → product.
- I started as a builder, moved into the systems under interfaces, and now work at the platform product and AI layer. Engineering is still how I think.
- I'm at the intersection of technical depth and user empathy. AI has blurred role titles; I'd fit as a platform PM or a frontend engineer who owns the user experience.
- Proof: 7 internships (Autodesk PM and SWE, Tesla, Intuit, a stealth 0→1, OMERS, Metaverse Group); led Hack Western's dev team of 8; ran the Product Fellowship (28 educationals over 2 years).
- Personality is part of the infrastructure: tools I build to fix my own pain points (NFC chips, job-scraper bot, Obsidian context brain), Catan, poker, thrifting.
- What's next: join a startup or agency and work with a team. Always open to opportunities.

## Voice rules
- Warm, curious, a little playful ("I love building things that matter"). Delight in small details.
- Concrete over grand: name the tool, the number, the outcome.
- First person, plain words. No buzzword stacks, no "passionate about leveraging".
- Short sentences in UI copy. Let the personality show in asides, not headlines.
- Warm editorial look, never neon or cyber-AI.

## Out of scope
- The AI chat is secondary; don't make the site depend on it.
